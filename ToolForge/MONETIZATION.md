# Monetization and creator payments

ToolForge is designed to help independent developers publish software and receive support from buyers. The implemented product flow is a one-time digital purchase. Recurring memberships or monthly pledges are not implemented.

The app records a configurable platform fee and a creator earnings amount in its order ledger. These figures do not move money and must not be described as a payout or escrow service.

Real marketplace payouts require Razorpay Route approval, linked-account onboarding, transfer creation and reconciliation, refund and reversal handling, and merchant policy review. Do not advertise creator payouts until that work is complete and tested in Razorpay Test Mode.
